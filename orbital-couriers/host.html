How to play (30 seconds)
  1. Contracts wait at planets. Stop next to the pickup planet to load one (if you have room); stop next to its destination to deliver and score. Whoever picks it up first owns it.
  2. Each round, secretly program 3 moves on your phone. Your phone shows where you'll end up.
  3. The TV flies everyone at once, one move at a time. Lowest score goes first.
  4. Gravity wells pull you 1 space toward their core when you stop on an arrow. A Burn that starts inside a well gets +1 space.
  5. Fly into a ship and you bump it 1 space and take its spot.
  6. Deliveries pay ★ points and the same in ⬢ credits. Spend credits on upgrades; leftovers are worth 1★ per 4 at the end. Most ★ after round 8 wins.
