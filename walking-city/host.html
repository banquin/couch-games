How to play (30 seconds)
  1. Each of you runs one district of a city on legs. Each round you get 5 cards on your phone.
  2. Build 1, pass 1 left and 1 right, and you may keep 1 for next round. After round 1 you can only build cards your neighbours passed you (or the one you kept), so pass what they need!
  3. Buildings feed every touching tile, even across the border into a neighbour's district. A building missing an input goes dark.
  4. Engines turn Fuel + Power into strides. Workshops in the row touching the core deliver Parts to the Main Drive: when every district has delivered, the city gets permanent extra strides.
  5. When someone builds a card you passed, you get a Part crate. Crates can go to the Main Drive or keep a starving engine running.
  6. Reach the Haven before the storm catches you.
